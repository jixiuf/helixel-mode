* Action cycle — reselect spans of past motions:
#+begin_src text
  w w          forward two words
  ;            select full span of w w
  j j          down two lines
  ;            select full span of j j
  ;            select full span of w w (older)

  b b          backward two words
  C-;          mark start of b b (no full span)

  f w          find-char:"w"
  n n n        repeat of next find-char
  ;            select full span of find-char
hello world foo
hello world foo
hello world foo
hello world foo
hello world foo
#+end_src
